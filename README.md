## What it is

Make PPTX is a set of three *skills*, instructions and tools that teach an AI assistant such as Claude or Codex to make presentations. You give it your notes; you get back a PowerPoint deck that looks designed, not generated.

Each skill does one job, and you only use the ones you need:

```text
your notes  →  Make_Clean  →  Make_Style  →  Make_Colors  →  your deck
               form           look           brand
```

* * *

## The three skills

| **Make_Clean_PPTX** | **Make_Style_PPTX** | **Make_Colors_PPTX** |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **Form.** One message per slide, a matching design and chart from a library of 54 layouts and 60 visuals, checked before delivery. Black, grey and white. | **Look.** The same deck in another style: editorial, organic, geometric, tech, soft. Your content and layout stay as they are. | **Brand.** Your colours, fonts and logo, taken from your brand guidelines, your website or an example file, with readable contrast. |
| [**Available**](https://github.com/MaximeRumpler/Make_Clean_PPTX) · [Gallery](https://maximerumpler.github.io/Make_Clean_PPTX/) | In preparation | In preparation |

**Start with Make_Clean_PPTX**: it is all you need for a clean, professional deck, and the other two build on it.

* * *

## Install

Make_Clean_PPTX installs in one step in Claude Code, Claude.ai, Claude desktop and other AI assistants, on Windows, macOS and Linux: see [its install section](https://github.com/MaximeRumpler/Make_Clean_PPTX#install). The other skills will install the same way.

Then just ask, in your own words:

```text
Make a 10-slide deck from this report for the board.
Make an 8-slide deck in French from these notes.
```

* * *

## Principles

| **Your content stays yours** | **Designed, not guessed** | **Easy for everyone** |
|:---|:---|:---|
| No invented figures, quotes or names. The decks you make are yours, with no obligation. | Slides come from tested libraries, then are checked for overflow, density and readability. | Plain requests in English or French, one-step install, clear messages when something is missing. |

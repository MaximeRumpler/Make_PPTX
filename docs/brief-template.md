# Brief template

Fill in what you know, delete the rest, and paste it into your assistant with your notes or files. Every line is optional: the skill asks about what matters and picks sensible defaults for the rest. A filled brief simply gets you the right deck on the first try.

[Get started](get-started.md) explains how to install the skill and what each choice changes.

* * *

```text
Make a deck with the make-clean-pptx skill from this brief.

Audience:        (who will see it, and what they already know)
Goal:            (what they should decide, approve, understand or do afterwards)
Main message:    (the one sentence they should remember)
Slides:          (about how many, e.g. 8; or "as few as possible")
Language:        (English, French...)
Format:          (PowerPoint .pptx / LibreOffice .odp / both)
Confidentiality: (none / internal / confidential / I can't share the real content)
Accessibility:   (standard / extra care)
Tone:            (factual, persuasive, friendly, formal...)

Parts and key messages:
1. (part name): (the point it makes)
2. (part name): (the point it makes)
3. (part name): (the point it makes)

Figures (with their source):
- (figure, unit, period): (source, date)
- (figure, unit, period): (source, date)

Slides I need:
- (e.g. a cover with the date, an agenda, one slide per option, a timeline, next steps, a contact slide)

Extras:
- (speaker notes / icons / photos / none)

Constraints:
- (e.g. no photos, a 10-minute slot, names to leave out)

My content:
(paste your notes, report or table here, or attach the files)
```

* * *

## Tips

- **The main message matters most.** If you write only one line, write that one.
- **Give figures with their source.** The skill never invents a number; anything missing becomes a text slide or a question.
- **"Parts" are not slides.** Three parts may give six slides; the skill splits them so each slide carries one message.
- **Slides stay short.** Details that do not fit a slide go to the speaker notes, so nothing you gave is lost.
- **Can't share the content?** Choose *I can't share the real content* and describe the structure instead ("slide 3: one key figure"). You get a skeleton to fill in yourself.
